# Kelly → Matt SmartSite Architecture — Curated Source Extract

Original source: ChatGPT File Library conversations created primarily 2026-06-15 through 2026-07-03 concerning Kelly v1/v2 and Matt.

## Correct architectural parallel

The source explicitly corrects an earlier mistake and establishes the intended analogy:

Sarah → James is the phone-call model.
Kelly → Matt is the website-visitor model.

Kelly engages the website visitor first. Matt is not a direct-inbound entry point in the intended SmartSite flow.

## Kelly variants

Kelly v1: optional Matt transfer. If no form/survey has committed the prospect to a Matt demo, Kelly demonstrates the AI Smart Talking and Selling Website and asks whether the prospect wants the deeper AI sales-rep demo. If the prospect declines, Kelly continues into post-demo sales/closing rather than dead-ending the conversation.

Kelly v2: assumed Matt transfer. If the prospect completed the appropriate form/survey for the Kelly+Matt demo, Kelly performs her demo/light sale and treats transfer to Matt as the natural next step, subject to frustration/refusal/fallback rules.

## Matt's role

Matt is the deeper AI sales-rep demo/closer for the SmartSite experience. The source repeatedly clarifies that Matt 'turbo charges' the AI Smart Talking and Selling Website with deeper sales skills.

During roleplay Matt becomes the prospect's AI Sales Rep for the prospect's own business and handles a simulated hot website visitor that Kelly has already engaged. He should use the prospect's actual business/offer/customer context and reference Kelly's prior engagement naturally.

A corrected demo opening pattern in the historical source is conceptually:

Kelly was speaking with the visitor on the prospect's website, indicated the visitor was looking into the relevant offer and had additional questions, and Matt continues as the business's AI Sales Rep.

The visitor did not 'reach out' by phone; they were on the website and Kelly engaged them first.

## Demo/persona separation

During the live demo Matt stays in character as the prospect's AI Sales Rep/customer-facing specialist. When the demo ends, he steps clearly back into demo/closer mode. Do not blend personas mid-demo.

## Two-tier discovery requirement

SmartSite has two distinct discovery layers that must never be flattened:

1. Simulated visitor/customer discovery during Kelly/Matt roleplay — what the prospect's customers care about, pain, urgency, objections, decision drivers and desired outcomes.
2. Actual prospect/business-owner discovery during demo debrief and sales conversation — the owner's operational pain, hot buttons, emotions, desired future state, buying drivers, plan interest and objections.

Both layers can inform the sale, but they require separate provenance and entity/session relationships.

## Historical draft conflicts

Historical Matt drafts contain multiple superseded elements, including direct-inbound call framing, $177/$247 pricing, minimum-three-hot-button trial-close rules and yes/no-style trial-close examples. These must not be silently carried forward.

Current higher-precedence rules are in `current-overrides.md`, including $197/$297 SmartSite pricing, no included/unlimited-minutes claims, layered emotional trial-close requirements, and high-intent momentum rules.

## Field/data dependency evidence

Historical SmartSite work identifies fields such as AI website tools interest, AI availability need, current website ownership/access, AI success definition, automation workflow needs, payment/ecommerce need, approved phrases, future-state language and other visitor/business fields.

The source also identifies a recurring implementation mistake: Matt was initially seeded from `visitor_*` fields that Kelly was not populating. The correction was to have Kelly collect/retrieve the necessary visitor context or use authoritative business/contact context so Matt does not depend on blank fields.

## Closing requirement

The user explicitly called out that Matt must not merely demonstrate the product. After roleplay and debrief, Matt must sell, handle objections, recommend the appropriate plan and push toward subscription when the prospect remains qualified/interested. When no transfer occurs, Kelly may assume this downstream sales/closing responsibility.
